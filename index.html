Turn on what the agent may touch.

Renames go through the language server

workspace/willRenameFiles: re-exports, barrel files and aliased imports update before the file moves.

Search runs inside the process

36 tools and 12 Rust crates in-process, no fork per call, on machines with no rg and no grep.

Reviews come back ranked

/review sorts findings P0 to P3 and ends on a verdict instead of a wall of comments.

Parallel work stays in worktrees

Subagents get their own isolated worktree and return schema-validated results, never a dirty tree.

Debuggers attach, not describe

lldb, dlv and debugpy over DAP: breakpoints, frames and locals, on the real binary.
